Privacy policy

Blocks Publishing Ltd is the data controller for the personal data described here, in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. You can reach us at hello@blockspublishing.com.

What we collect

Order details (name, delivery address, email and order contents), payment details (processed securely by Stripe - we never see or store full card numbers), the email you give us for the free resources pack, and anything you send us via a form.

Cookies and analytics

We keep cookies to a minimum and ask before setting any that aren't essential. Essential storage (your basket and cookie choice) is always on. Google Analytics 4 runs only if you accept, via Google Consent Mode. Stripe's checkout pages use their own cookies for security.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can access, correct, delete or port your data, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. Every marketing email includes a one-click unsubscribe.
